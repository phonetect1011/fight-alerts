'''Joshua Felix Pacio'''<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.onefc.com/world-champions/joshua-pacio/|title= Joshua "The Passion" Pacio|date=2019|website=onefc.com|accessdate=14 November 2020}}</ref> (born January 10, 1996) is a Filipino [[Mixed martial arts|mixed martial artist]] who competes in the [[Strawweight (MMA)|Strawweight]] division of [[ONE Championship]]. He is the three-time and current [[List of ONE Championship champions#Strawweight World Championship|ONE Strawweight World Champion]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.abs-cbn.com/amp/sports/04/12/19/mma-joshua-pacio-retakes-one-strawweight-title-in-sensational-ko-win|title=MMA: Joshua Pacio retakes ONE strawweight title in sensational KO win|website=news. abs-cbn.com|language=en|access-date=2020-11-14}}</ref>

==Early life and education==
Joshua Pacio is of [[Igorot people|Igorot]] descent, growing up in [[La Trinidad, Benguet|La Trinidad]], [[Benguet]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Joshua Pacio Embodies The Igorot Spirit |url=https://www.onefc.com/news/joshua-pacio-is-proud-of-his-igorot-warrior-spirit/ |access-date=8 December 2024 |work=ONE Championship |date=12 September 2018}}</ref> He had to learn to be independent at an early age after his father worked overseas to provide for his family. Pacio got exposed to martial arts at the early age of 11, where he started working on his kickboxing, before transitioning to Wushu at age 13.<ref>{{cite web |title=Joshua "The Passion" Pacio |url=https://www.onefc.com/athletes/joshua-pacio/ |website=ONE Championship – The Home Of Martial Arts |date=31 January 2017 |access-date=15 April 2021}}</ref> Pacio took up Hotel and Restaurant Management at the [[University of the Cordilleras]] in [[Baguio]]<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://sports.abs-cbn.com/mma/news/2016/10/05/pacio-getting-done-inside-cage-classroom-16253|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161009131716/http://sports.abs-cbn.com/mma/news/2016/10/05/pacio-getting-done-inside-cage-classroom-16253|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 9, 2016|title=Sports}}</ref>

==Mixed martial arts career==

===Early mixed martial arts career===

Pacio made his debut at Team Lakay Championship 8,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.sherdog.com/events/TLC-Team-Lakay-Championship-8-34727|title=TLC - Team Lakay Championship 8|website=Sherdog|accessdate=25 October 2020}}</ref> where he came away with a first-round TKO over Denver Songaben. This would be a start off red-hot six-match winning streak which eventually led to him being signed by [[ONE Championship]].

He made his ONE Championship debut on April 15, 2016 - beating compatriot Rabin Catalan by second-round technical knockout at [[2016 in ONE Championship#ONE Championship: Global Rivals|ONE Championship: Global Rivals]] at the [[Mall of Asia Arena]] in the Philippines.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.abs-cbn.com/sports/04/16/16/pinoy-fighters-battle-in-one-global-rivals|title=Pinoy fighters battle in 'ONE: Global Rivals'|date=16 April 2016|website=ABS-CBN News|access-date=25 October 2020}}</ref>
 
After beating [[Kritsada Kongsrichai]], Pacio earned his first shot at the [[List of ONE Championship champions#Strawweight World Championship|ONE Strawweight World Championship]] against [[Yoshitaka Naito]] at [[2016 in ONE Championship#ONE Championship: State of Warriors|ONE Championship: State of Warriors]] on October 17, 2016, in [[Yangon]].

Pacio came close to being a World Champion at age 20, as he punished Naito with his patented Wushu strikes on the feet, but a mishap in the third round cost him the entire match as he got submitted by the Japanese via rear naked choke.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2018/08/yoshitaka-naito-defends-strawweight-title-vs-joshua-pacio-at-one-championship-80|title=Yoshitaka Naito defends strawweight title vs. Joshua Pacio at ONE Championship 80|date=17 August 2018|accessdate=25 October 2020}}</ref>

===First world title reign===

After his loss to Naito, Pacio would wreak havoc in the division - winning five of his next six bouts to book another date with Naito this time at ONE: Conquest of Heroes on September 23, 2018, in Jakarta Indonesia.

Pacio rematched [[Yoshitaka Naito]] at [[2018 in ONE Championship#ONE Championship: Conquest of Heroes|ONE Championship: Conquest of Heroes]] on September 22, 2018. Pacio looked more confident in the rematch as he stuffed Naito's takedowns for much of the first two rounds while punishing him on the striking department with lightning quick kicks and combinations from different angles.
